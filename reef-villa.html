01 The property

Gardens, lily ponds and a long sandy beach.

Set on 434 perches of tropical gardens and lily ponds with a 65 metre beach frontage, opened in 2008 and registered with the Tourist Board. Seven suites of 95 to 125 square metres, a triple height Plantation House, the Summer Breeze restaurant, spa, shop and pavilion.

Finishes in marble, granite, sandstone and limestone, with Sri Lankan antique windows, columns and doors worked into the design. Direct access from the gardens to the beach, without cliffs or a railway line to cross.

02 The situation

An established property, brought to market, requiring capital and a plan.

The asset

Valued at LKR 604 million by a chartered valuer in 2024. Asset rich, well maintained, with a loyal professional staff and maintenance records available for inspection.

The constraint

Seven keys limits turnover, and restricts the tour operators willing to sell the property. Revenue is concentrated in a small inventory and a seasonal market.

The investor

Acquisition through a newly formed company. USD 663,000 equity at twenty per cent, and a USD 2.66 million DFCC Bank facility at eight per cent over eight years, with a grace period on capital in year one.

Our appointment

Templetrees International appointed to manage the property, reposition it in the market, and deliver the staged expansion to fifteen keys.
