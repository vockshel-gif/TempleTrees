05 How an engagement runs

The ideal roadmap.

Five steps, the same for every owner. We say plainly what we believe is achievable, and what sits outside our scope.

01

Assessment

We review the property, its market and its financials, and set out honestly what we believe is achievable.

02

Agreement

Scope, fee, term and responsibilities are agreed in writing, including what sits outside our scope.

03

Mobilisation

Systems, people and procedures are put in place. For a new property this is the pre-opening programme.

04

Operation

The property runs to agreed standards against an agreed budget, with monthly reporting to the owner.

05

Review

Performance is measured against the plan, and the plan is revised for the year ahead.
